## English

### What's new

- **Device upload/download speeds.** The overview can display per-device sampled average speeds from an existing `nlbwmon` collector. Readings follow its configured collection interval; initial samples, missing records and counter resets show `—`. Reads pause while the page is hidden, and DHCP lease information continues to update independently.
- **Realtime graphs.** Fix LuCI's load, bandwidth and connection graphs failing to fill the available width after collapsing or expanding the sidebar or resizing the window. Existing samples and polling are preserved, and labels keep their normal size.
- **Disk mounting dropdowns.** Fix DiskMan mount options being clipped at the bottom of the table. Open menus can extend beyond table and phone cell boundaries, while closed tables retain their original layout and rounded clipping.

### Upgrade notes

- After upgrading, log out of LuCI and back in once so the new read-only device traffic permissions take effect.
- Device speeds require an available `nlbwmon` collector. Carbon uses its existing settings and does not install or start it. Only traffic observed by this router is included; bridge traffic, offloaded traffic and traffic through other gateways may be missing. Systems without a supported collector retain the device list without speed readings.

## 简体中文

### 更新内容

- **设备上下行网速。** 概览可读取已有 `nlbwmon` 采集服务的数据，显示各设备的采样平均网速。读取频率遵循采集服务的现有周期；首次采样、记录缺失或计数重置时显示 `—`。页面隐藏时暂停读取，DHCP 租约信息仍独立刷新。
- **实时图表。** 修复 LuCI 的负载、带宽和连接数图表在折叠、展开侧栏或调整窗口大小后未铺满可用宽度的问题。保留已有采样和持续刷新，文字标签保持正常尺寸。
- **磁盘挂载下拉框。** 修复 DiskMan 挂载选项在表格底部被裁切的问题。展开的菜单可越过表格及手机端单元格边界，关闭后保留原有布局和圆角裁切。

### 升级须知

- 升级后请退出 LuCI 并重新登录一次，让新增的设备流量只读权限生效。
- 设备网速依赖可用的 `nlbwmon` 采集服务。Carbon 沿用其现有设置，不会安装或启动该服务。仅统计本机观测到的流量，桥接、流量卸载及经过其他网关的流量可能不完整。不支持采集服务的系统保留设备列表，不显示网速。

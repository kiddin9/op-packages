## English

### What's new

- **Side-router device discovery.** Fix LAN neighbours being omitted when LAN obtains its own address by DHCP. Device discovery continues to combine DHCP leases, Wi-Fi associations and IPv4 ARP neighbours with the local DHCP server disabled. Recognisable separate uplinks are excluded; ambiguous custom interfaces are retained. Addresses [issue #3](https://github.com/mrjoechen/luci-theme-carbon/issues/3).
- **Clearer device information.** The overview now says "Discovered devices": lease records and neighbour caches may include offline devices and do not represent every device on the network. Gateways, neighbour entries and DHCP leases are labelled separately, instead of assuming that a missing local lease means a fixed IP. Labels are translated into all bundled languages and wrap on phones to preserve room for device names.
- **Dashboard data permissions.** Allow ARP and temperature readings through their resolved procfs/sysfs paths, fixing missing data on systems whose rpcd checks those paths.
- **Traffic selector.** Use the Carbon listbox for the live-traffic interface picker, with grouped choices and keyboard support. Listboxes stay open when a page update triggers a scroll without moving the control.
- **Layout.** Use more space for dashboards and tables on wide screens, with two columns of devices where space permits. Improve phone layouts for menus, tables and dashboard content.
- **Device brands.** Show brand logos in LuCI's native lease tables as well as the overview, and add Midea and Haier device badges.
- **Dark mode.** Fix the colour of enabled switches.

### Upgrade notes

- After upgrading, log out of LuCI and back in once so the updated dashboard permissions take effect, especially if ARP devices or temperatures are missing.
- "Discovered devices" is based on data visible to this router. It may contain offline records and cannot guarantee a complete live count of the network.

## 简体中文

### 更新内容

- **旁路由设备发现。** 修复 LAN 自身通过 DHCP 获取地址时，邻居设备被遗漏的问题。关闭本机 DHCP 服务后，仍通过 DHCP 租约、Wi-Fi 关联列表与 IPv4 ARP 邻居合并发现设备；排除可识别的独立上联接口，对角色不明确的自定义接口保留发现结果。对应 [issue #3](https://github.com/mrjoechen/luci-theme-carbon/issues/3)。
- **设备信息更准确。** 概览改为“已发现设备”：租约和邻居缓存可能包含离线设备，也不代表全网所有设备。明确区分网关、邻居记录和 DHCP 租约，不再将缺少本机租约的设备一律标为固定 IP。同步全部内置语言，手机端标签换行，避免挤压设备名称。
- **仪表盘数据权限。** 补充 ARP 与温度文件解析后的 procfs/sysfs 实际路径权限，修复部分 rpcd 版本下无法读取数据的问题。
- **流量接口选择。** 实时流量的接口选择改为 Carbon 样式列表，支持选项分组与键盘操作；页面更新引起滚动、但控件位置未改变时，下拉列表不再意外关闭。
- **布局优化。** 宽屏下的仪表盘和表格充分利用空间，设备列表在空间足够时显示为两列；改善手机端菜单、表格与仪表盘内容的布局。
- **设备品牌。** LuCI 原生租约表也显示品牌图标，新增美的和海尔设备标识。
- **深色模式。** 修复开关启用状态的颜色。

### 升级须知

- 升级后请退出 LuCI 并重新登录一次，让更新后的仪表盘权限生效；尤其适用于 ARP 设备或温度信息缺失的情况。
- “已发现设备”来自本机可见的数据，可能包含离线记录，不能保证完整的全网实时在线数量。
